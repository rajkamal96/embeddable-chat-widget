# New patients

## Your first visit

A first visit takes about 60 minutes. It includes a full examination, X-rays if you need them, a cleaning when time allows, and a plan explained in plain language. You will get a written quote for any treatment before it begins.

## What to bring

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, a list of any medicines you take, and any recent dental X-rays from your previous dentist.

## Forms

You can fill in the new patient form online before your visit, or arrive 10 minutes early and complete it at the front desk.

## Cancellations

Please give us at least 24 hours notice if you need to change or cancel an appointment. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged a $40 fee.
