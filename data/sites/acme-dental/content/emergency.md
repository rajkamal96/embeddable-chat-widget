# Dental emergencies

## Same-day emergency appointments

If you have severe tooth pain, a broken or knocked-out tooth, or a swollen face, call us as early as you can. We keep a few same-day emergency slots each weekday, and calls before 10:00 am have the best chance of getting one.

## Outside opening hours

Our voicemail is checked every evening and every Saturday morning. If swelling affects your breathing or swallowing, or after a serious injury, go to the nearest hospital emergency department or call your local emergency number.
