# Pricing

All prices are estimates in US dollars and may change after an examination. You will always get a written quote before treatment starts.

## Price list

- Check-up with X-rays: $95
- Professional cleaning: $120
- Composite filling: from $150 per tooth
- In-office teeth whitening: $349
- Take-home whitening kit: $199
- Root canal treatment: from $750
- Clear aligners: free consultation, treatment from $2,800
- Dental implant: consultation $75, implant and crown from $2,400
- Children's check-up and cleaning (under 12): $85

## Payment plans

For treatment over $500 we offer interest-free payment plans over 6 or 12 months. Ask the front desk to set one up before your treatment starts.
