# Insurance and payment

## Insurance

We are in-network with SunShield Dental and BlueHarbor Dental plans. For other plans we can give you an itemised receipt to claim back yourself. Please bring your insurance card to your first visit so we can check your cover.

## Ways to pay

We accept major credit and debit cards, bank transfer, and HSA or FSA cards. We do not accept cheques. Payment is due at the time of treatment unless you have agreed a payment plan.
