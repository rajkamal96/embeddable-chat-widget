# Frequently asked questions

## Is treatment painful?

We use local anaesthetic for fillings, root canals and implants, and we explain each step before we start. Tell us at any time if you want a break.

## Do you help nervous patients?

Yes. We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for anxious patients and longer appointments so nobody feels rushed. We do not offer general anaesthesia or IV sedation.

## How often should I have a check-up?

Most patients should have a check-up and cleaning every six months. The dentist may suggest a different schedule for you.

## Do you see emergencies?

Yes, we keep same-day emergency slots on weekdays. See the Dental emergencies page.

## Can I bring my child?

Yes. We see children from age 2, and a parent stays with the child during the visit.
