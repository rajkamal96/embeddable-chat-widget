# Hours and location

## Opening hours

- Monday to Thursday: 8:00 am to 5:00 pm
- Friday: 8:00 am to 2:00 pm
- Saturday: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm, by appointment only
- Sunday: closed

We are closed on public holidays.

## Find us

Acme Dental is at 214 Maple Street, Springfield. The building is wheelchair accessible. There is free parking behind the building, and the nearest bus stop is Maple and 3rd.
