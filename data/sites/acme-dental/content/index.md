# Acme Dental

Acme Dental is a friendly family dental practice in Springfield. We look after children, adults and anxious patients, and we aim to keep visits calm, clear and on time.

We offer check-ups and cleanings, fillings, teeth whitening, clear aligners, dental implants, root canal treatment and children's dentistry. Details and prices are on our Services and Pricing pages.
