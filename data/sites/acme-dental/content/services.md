# Our services

## Check-ups and cleaning

A routine check-up includes an examination, digital X-rays when needed, and advice on your home care. A professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar. We suggest a check-up and cleaning every six months for most patients.

## Fillings

We repair cavities with tooth-coloured composite fillings that match your natural teeth. Most fillings take one visit of about 45 minutes.

## Teeth whitening

We offer two options: in-office whitening, done in one visit of about 75 minutes, and a take-home whitening kit with custom trays that you use for two weeks. Whitening is not suitable for everyone, and the dentist will check your teeth and gums first.

## Clear aligners

Clear aligners are removable plastic trays that gradually straighten teeth. Dr. Tom Alvarez plans every case. Treatment usually takes between 6 and 18 months. We do not currently offer metal braces.

## Dental implants

Dental implants replace a missing tooth with a titanium post and a crown. The process takes several months because the bone needs time to heal. A consultation with a 3D scan is needed before we can confirm a plan.

## Root canal treatment

Root canal treatment removes infected tissue from inside a tooth to save it. Most root canals are completed in one or two visits, using local anaesthetic.

## Children's dentistry

We see children from age 2. Visits are short and gentle, with fluoride treatment and sealants available. Parents stay with their child during the visit.
